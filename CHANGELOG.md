# Changelog

Download any version from [Releases](https://github.com/CAITOUS/JobTailor/releases). To update, back up, replace the files in your Job Tailor folder, then click **Reload** on the extension card. Never click Remove.

## 1.3.0 · November 2026

- **Clickable links.** Email, LinkedIn and website in the resume and cover letter are real hyperlinks. The full address stays visible as text, on its own line, so applicant tracking systems read it and long URLs don't wrap.
- **Optional QR codes.** Options › Your details › **Add QR codes for LinkedIn and website**. The resume header becomes three columns: name and contact (60%), then LinkedIn and Portfolio QR codes (20% each) with vertical labels. Off by default; best for printed copies.
- QR codes are generated in the extension, with no outside service or library.

## 1.2.0 · November 2026

**Application tracking.** The Log tab is now a follow-up tracker.

- **Stages:** tailored, applied, screening, interviewing, offer, accepted, rejected, withdrawn, no response. Every change goes on the job's timeline.
- **Per job:** next follow-up date, next action, contact, their role, contact info, pay range and notes.
- **Log an activity:** follow-up sent, they responded, recruiter/phone screen, interview, thank-you, offer, rejection or note, with method (email, LinkedIn, phone, text, portal, video, in person, referral), date, who it was with and details. Each one suggests the next follow-up and stage.
- **Timeline** per job, with numbered follow-ups.
- **Due first:** jobs needing a follow-up sort to the top and are flagged. The count shows on the Log tab and the toolbar icon.
- **Filters and search** across jobs, contacts and notes.
- **Exports:** Export CSV (one row per job, with the full timeline) and Activity CSV (one row per activity).
- Existing log entries upgrade automatically. Restoring a backup merges timelines.
- New permission: **alarms**, to keep the toolbar count current.
