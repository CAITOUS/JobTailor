Job Tailor Options

Claude API

Stored only in this browser profile. Create a key at console.anthropic.com and set a monthly spend limit there.

Your details (used on documents and autofill)

Career source files

Load .md or .txt files: resumes, role histories, accomplishment lists, display rules. Claude writes only from these. Save a .docx as plain text first (Word: File › Save As › Plain Text).

Rules

One per line. These override everything else in the prompt.

Known answers (optional)

Facts you're comfortable reusing on applications. Anything not here or in your sources comes back as “NEEDS YOU”.

Log

Used for the first follow-up after you apply and after each follow-up you log. Interviews, screens and offers suggest shorter gaps; you can change any date on the Log tab.

Backup & restore

Your settings, source files, added context, and log live in this browser profile. They carry over when you update the extension, but are deleted if you remove it. Keep a backup.

Restoring merges: nothing already here is deleted, and the backup wins where the same item exists in both.
